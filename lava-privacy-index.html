Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

This policy explains what information the Lava Player app for Android (the “app”) handles and why. The app is made by inflavor studio (“we”, “us”).

Your music stays on your device

Lava Player plays audio files that are already stored on your device. With your permission it reads your audio library through Android’s media storage. Your music files, their tags, covers and lyrics, your edits to them, playlists, favorites and all app settings are stored only on your device. We never upload them and have no access to them.

Analytics

To understand how many people use the app and which features they use, Lava Player uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service provided by Google. It collects the following automatically:

  • an app instance identifier and, if your device settings allow it, the Android advertising ID;
  • device information: device model, Android version, language, screen size;
  • app information: app version, when the app was first opened and updated;
  • usage: number and length of sessions, screens viewed and time spent in the app;
  • approximate location (country or region), derived from your IP address.

This data is not linked to your name, email or any account: the app has no accounts and never asks for personal details. We use it only for analytics, to improve the app. The app shows no ads, and we do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Google processes this data on our behalf and receives it over an encrypted connection. We keep analytics data for no longer than 14 months. How Google handles it is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Uninstalling the app stops all data collection and removes everything the app stored on your device.
  • You can ask us to delete analytics data related to you by writing to the address below.

Permissions

  • Music and audio: to find and play audio files on your device.
  • Notifications: to show playback controls in the notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • Internet: used only to send analytics data.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on, your device may back up the app’s settings to your Google account. This backup is managed by Android and Google under your account’s settings; we have no access to it.

Links to other websites

The app contains links to Google Play, Ko-fi and community pages (Reddit, Discord, Telegram, Instagram). They open in your browser or in those apps, and those services have their own privacy policies.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
