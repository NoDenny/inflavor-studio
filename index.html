Lava Player

Your music, reimagined. A music player for the files already on your phone. Free to use, no ads, no accounts, no subscriptions. Just your library, played well.

  • Speed & pitch

    Slow a track down or speed it up by up to 12 semitones either way, with one swipe on the pitch badge. Changes apply instantly, without a pause.

  • Interactive visualizer

    A living scene that moves to the music and responds to your touch. Customize it and play with it while you listen.

  • Minimal, gesture-based

    Nothing extra on the screen. Pause, seek, skip and change the pitch with gestures.

  • Covers, names & lyrics

    Set your own artwork, title, artist and lyrics for any track, and see how your music looks in a player.

  • Your library

    Albums, artists, playlists, Favorites and search. All popular audio formats are supported.
